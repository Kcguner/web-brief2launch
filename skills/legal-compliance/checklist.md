# Legal checklist — jurisdictions and page contents

> Not legal advice. Confirm every item with a qualified lawyer in the relevant
> jurisdiction.

## Jurisdiction triggers

| Regime | Triggered when | Core obligation |
|--------|----------------|-----------------|
| KVKK (Türkiye) | Data subjects in Türkiye | Aydınlatma metni, explicit consent where required, VERBİS registration in some cases |
| GDPR (EU/EEA) | Offering goods/services to EU/EEA residents or monitoring their behavior | Lawful basis, transparency, consent for non-essential cookies, DSRs, DPA with processors |
| UK GDPR | Same for the UK | Same as GDPR, with the ICO as authority |
| CCPA/CPRA (California) | For-profit business meeting revenue or data-volume thresholds | Notice at collection, opt-out of sale/share, DSRs |
| LGPD (Brazil) | Data subjects in Brazil | Legal basis, transparency, DSRs |
| PIPEDA (Canada) | Commercial activity in Canada | Consent, access, limited collection |

When several apply, satisfy the strictest requirement that covers each data
category.

## Data-flow inventory fields

For each flow record: name, data categories, purpose, legal basis, controller
and processor, storage location, retention, recipients, transfer mechanism,
and how consent is obtained.

## Privacy policy contents

- Identity and contact details of the controller.
- Categories of data collected and the source.
- Purposes and legal bases.
- Recipients and international transfers with safeguards.
- Retention periods.
- Data-subject rights and how to exercise them.
- Complaint route to the supervisory authority.
- Cookies summary with a link to the settings.
- Last-updated date and version.
- Children's data position if the audience may include minors.

## Cookie policy contents

- Categories: strictly necessary, preferences, analytics, marketing.
- Named cookies with provider, purpose, and lifetime.
- How to change or withdraw consent.
- Statement that non-essential cookies load only after opt-in.

## KVKK disclosure notice (aydınlatma metni) contents

- Data controller (veri sorumlusu) identity and contact.
- Purpose of processing personal data (kişisel verilerin işlenme amacı).
- Legal basis (hukuki sebep, KVKK art. 5/6).
- Recipient groups and transfers (alıcı grupları ve aktarım).
- Retention period (saklama süresi).
- Data-subject rights (KVKK art. 11 hakları) and the application route.
- VERBİS note when registration applies.

## Terms of use contents

- Who operates the site and how to contact them.
- Acceptable use and prohibited conduct.
- Accounts, credentials, and termination.
- Intellectual property and license scope.
- User-generated content license and moderation.
- Disclaimers and limitation of liability within legal limits.
- Dispute resolution and governing law.
- Changes to the terms and how they are announced.

## E-commerce extras

- Price, taxes, and currency presentation.
- Shipping times and costs.
- Right of withdrawal and its exceptions.
- Refund and return process and timelines.
- Order confirmation and invoice details.
- Consumer protection disclosures required by the target market.

## Consent implementation tests

1. Clear cookies and storage, load the site: no analytics or marketing cookie
   is set before any choice.
2. Reject all: no non-essential cookie is set, and the choice persists.
3. Accept all: expected cookies appear and are documented in the cookie policy.
4. Accept only analytics: marketing scripts stay blocked.
5. Withdraw consent: previously set non-essential cookies are removed or
   invalidated.
6. Consent record: the timestamp, version, and categories chosen are stored.

## Record keeping

- Keep a record of processing activities for each processing operation.
- Keep the consent log with version and timestamp.
- Keep the DPA signed copy for each processor.
- Keep the breach log and the notification decision for each incident.
